Career

1. Qualified Person (QP) & Quality Assurance (QA) Managers

Qualified Persons hold ultimate legal responsibility for certifying that every individual batch of pharmaceuticals produced or imported meets strict regulatory specifications before public release. Quality Assurance managers oversee corporate compliance systems, audit batch records, and maintain standard operating procedures across all manufacturing units.

2. Quality Control (QC) Analytical Chemists

QC Chemists analyze active ingredient raw materials, intermediates, and final dosage forms using advanced analytical techniques like HPLC, UPLC, UV-Vis spectroscopy, and automated dissolution testing. Their work ensures chemical purity, potency, uniform disintegration, and physical stability.

3. Formulation & Process Validation Engineers

Chemical and pharmaceutical engineers design scale-up manufacturing operations, optimizing tablet compression, liquid filling, or drug delivery matrix formulation. Process validation specialists systematically prove that production equipment consistently yields identical quality standards across high-volume production batches.

4. Regulatory Affairs (RA) Specialists

RA Specialists build the crucial communication bridge between TurtlePharma and national or international health agencies (such as SÚKL or EMA). They prepare technical drug dossiers, manage product registration renewals, and verify packaging compliance.

5. Pharmacovigilance (PV) & Medical Safety Officers

Medical safety officers monitor ongoing post-marketing safety by recording, evaluating, and reporting any adverse events reported by physicians or patients. Their vigilant monitoring protects patient safety throughout the complete market lifecycle of every drug product.

6. Cold-Chain Logistics & Supply Chain Specialists

Logistics personnel coordinate secure, temperature-monitored storage and distribution networks. They ensure biologicals, vaccines, and sensitive oral medications remain strictly within required temperature bounds from chemical synthesis to hospital pharmacy shelf.
