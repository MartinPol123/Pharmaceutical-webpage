Good Manufacturing Practise and other necessary laws

Good Manufacturing Practice (GMP / cGMP)

Good Manufacturing Practice is a mandatory quality assurance system that ensures pharmaceutical products are consistently manufactured and tested according to strict quality standards. It minimizes health risks by requiring sterile facility environments, verified production procedures, and complete documentation for every medication batch.

Good Laboratory Practice (GLP)

Good Laboratory Practice outlines international guidelines for safety testing and non-clinical laboratory studies. It guarantees that laboratory experiments, analytical measurements, and safety assessments are conducted with high integrity and total data reproducibility.

Good Distribution Practice (GDP)

Good Distribution Practice regulates the handling, storage, and transportation of medicines from manufacturing plants to community pharmacies. It guarantees that pharmaceutical products remain uncompromised, unadulterated, and temperature-controlled throughout their journey.

European Pharmacopoeia Standards (Ph. Eur.)

The European Pharmacopoeia provides legal quality standards for active pharmaceutical ingredients, excipients, and final dosages across Europe. Adhering to these monographs ensures that all raw chemical materials meet verified standards of purity, identity, and strength.

Pharmacovigilance Legislation & Medical Device Directives

European drug safety laws require pharmaceutical distributors to maintain active monitoring systems for immediate reporting of suspected side effects. This continuous monitoring ensures rapid public health protection and transparent safety communication.
