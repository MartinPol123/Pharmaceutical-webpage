Turtle Scientific name Habitat Lifespan Important fact
Hermann's tortoise Testudo hermanni Southern Europe Several decades Terrestrial tortoise; requires suitable temperature, diet and UVB exposure.
Green sea turtle Chelonia mydas Tropical and subtropical oceans 70+ years possible Herbivorous adults often feed on seagrasses and algae.
Loggerhead turtle Caretta caretta Atlantic, Pacific and Indian Oceans 70+ years estimated Powerful jaws help it eat hard-shelled marine animals.

Important: Turtles and tortoises do not have a universal medication schedule. Their veterinary treatment depends on the species, environment, symptoms, diagnosis, and individual health. Proper nutrition, sanitation, temperature, habitat design, and veterinary care are important for reptile health.
